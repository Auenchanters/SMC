One probe.
One spot.

Every push into the soil takes 10 to 30 seconds.

A person cannot probe a whole field by hand. A rover could, if it knew where to probe.

What we built

  • agrisimA simulator of a planted tomato field, its soil, a rover and its probe
  • a moisture mapA few dozen readings become a map of the whole field, with its own uncertainty
  • agribenchStrategies scored against published methods, on fields nobody has seen
  • AgriRover v2A rover designed from the physics, and a 3D replay of every survey
